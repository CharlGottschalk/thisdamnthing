# Core skills

These skills come with every Dryft workspace; no optional stacks are required.
Use them to manage your workspace, knowledge, projects and workflows through your
agent. Select a skill below to read its usage guide. In Claude, invoke it with
`/` before its name; in Codex, use `$` or the skill picker.

- **[dryft-add-project](projects.md)** — Link an existing project without moving or changing its files, and get an explanation of its purpose, structure and entry points.
- **[dryft-constitution](constitution.md)** — Define or update your workspace's working rules and permission preferences, with review before saving changes.
- **[dryft-find-skills](skills.md)** — Find reusable workflows in accessible completed sessions, or save a workflow from your current work as a skill after your approval.
- **[dryft-install-stack](stacks.md#marketplace-discovery-and-installation)** — Find, inspect and install an optional stack from the marketplace or a local directory, with review of its source, requirements and executable content.
- **[dryft-remove-stack](stacks.md#approved-updates-and-complete-uninstall)** — Uninstall a selected stack while preserving your brain notes, linked projects and user-created work.
- **[dryft-review-brain](brain.md#review-proposals)** — Review proposed knowledge and choose what to approve, edit or reject. Edited proposals remain pending until approved.
- **[dryft-search](brain.md#search-and-follow-sources)** — Answer questions using approved brain knowledge, cite sources and explain missing or conflicting evidence.
- **[dryft-ui](ui.md)** — Use local browser interviews and interactive pages, submit answers and continue the work with your agent.
- **[dryft-update-stack](stacks.md#approved-updates-and-complete-uninstall)** — Inspect an available stack update and apply the exact replacement you approve.
- **[dryft-workspace](agent-bootstrap.md#open-the-workspace-and-review-trust)** — Get oriented in your workspace, check its setup and find installed stacks, skills and usage guides.
