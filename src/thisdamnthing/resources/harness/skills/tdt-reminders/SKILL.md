# Reminder router

Read `.tdt/skills/tdt-remind/SKILL.md` to create, list, edit, snooze, complete or
cancel reminders. Read `.tdt/skills/tdt-check-reminders/SKILL.md` to check and
display due reminders manually or through an authorized scheduler. Follow every
delivery, claim and review-turn rule in the selected specialist.
